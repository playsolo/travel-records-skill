# Mailbox discovery and receipt evidence

Search both event words and provider domains. A subject-only search misses receipts whose subjects are “We hope you enjoyed your stay”, “账单”, or an online check-in invitation. Useful scoped queries include:

```text
{from:hilton.com from:feedback.hilton.com}
{from:accor.com from:accor-mail.com from:accorhotels.com}
{receipt folio invoice bill factura recibo Rechnung 账单 帳單 水单 收据 收據 发票 發票} {hotel resort Marriott Hilton Hyatt Accor 酒店 飯店}
{reservation confirmation booking itinerary check-in checking 入住 预订 預訂 訂房 退房}
{subject:"出票成功确认" subject:"出票成功" subject:"出票通知" subject:"购票成功" subject:"电子客票"}
{from:trip.com from:ctrip.com} {水单 电子发票 机票 行程确认单 预订}
{from:ur.alitrip.com from:notice.alitrip.com} {入住凭证 行程确认单 航变 交易确认}
```

These are discovery hints, not an allowlist or proof that a message is authentic or relevant. Inspect actual senders and bodies, include hotel-specific domains when observed, paginate every query, and report the inspected scope. Do not exclude promotions by default: transactional messages can be misclassified. Do not claim a complete mailbox scan merely because all keyword result pages were exhausted.

For annual analysis, apply the requested mail-year bounds to every discovery and follow-up query, including exact-order searches. For example, a 2026 Gmail query keeps `after:2026/01/01 before:2027/01/01` along with its search terms; use the connector's date semantics and report them. Do not search 2025 mail simply because it could contain a 2026 reservation. Trip dates are separate extracted facts. Prefer message-scoped reads; if a connector expands a thread, exclude other-year messages and their attachments from this run. A quote inside an in-scope message remains part of that message, with its original date context preserved.

## Booking channels and unfamiliar templates

Use a shared evidence model across hotel/airline direct bookings, OTAs (including Ctrip/Trip.com and Fliggy), restaurant booking services, travel agents and manual correspondence. Adapt discovery terms and reading of a new layout without requiring a new brand-specific parser. Search the user's requested scope; a 2014–2019 evaluation does not cover 2023–2024 examples. Mail may arrive months after the trip, so do not confuse mail-date filtering with experience-date coverage.

- Ctrip hotel water bills (`酒店水单`) can state the guest, property, arrival/departure, rooms and an explicit room-charge total. Link the bill, invoice notification and attached VAT invoice through the actual order/financial identity. They can be three representations of one spend, not three stays or three expenses. An invoice's issue date is not the stay date; a tax invoice alone need not prove physical occupancy.
- Fliggy `入住凭证` / `交易确认` / `Hotel Voucher` confirms a booking. Read CHECK-IN/CHECK-OUT separately from Booking Date, and distinguish rooms, guests and nights. “Non-refundable” is a policy, not a cancellation. Adjacent nights on different orders remain separate orders; exact repeated voucher PDFs add provenance. A figure in a hotel tax-estimate paragraph is not automatically the booking's total price.
- Booking.com confirmations can show a total that includes taxes or a room price that excludes them. Keep the stated total and its scope; do not add included components again or invent a tax-inclusive total. A quoted currency can differ from the currency payable locally; preserve the quote without inventing a settlement exchange rate. Booking/PIN management links are credentials, not export evidence.
- A retailer confirmation can carry a separate supplier confirmation ID (for example, an agency booking fulfilled by another platform). Preserve the private ID namespaces and explicit relationship: two channel names/IDs are not automatically two orders.
- An attachment-only confirmation still needs its PDF read. A pre-stay “Reservation Receipt” with “paid in full” supports a prepayment, not completed occupancy. Promised rewards and later “sent for posting” rewards are not money or proof of actual airline-account posting; match related evidence by explicit transaction identity.
- Agency flight confirmations can contain two passenger tickets for one flight. Extract the owner's segment once, preserve co-traveler context, and distinguish per-passenger ticket face totals from the order total after discounts/fees. Do not add both price bases together. Use actual itinerary airports; a city-level heading can retain CTU while the segment explicitly departs TFU. A date showing only day/month must not silently acquire a year from the mail/issue date; preserve the partial detail and seek a fully dated source.
- Mail-app generated travel cards are discovery clues, not canonical provider evidence. They can misread numeric month/day dates, show a superseded flight, or disagree on arrival time. Resolve dates against named-month original itineraries, tickets and invoices; preserve cross-midnight arrival dates and long gaps between separately listed legs. Do not force a later June leg into a January connection. A year explicitly supplied by the original itinerary subject can provide that itinerary year; retain its provenance rather than using the send year.
- Agency confirmation, eTicket and invoice can all document one flight. Use explicit booking/ticket identity to link them and keep one cash amount per underlying transaction. For an explicitly revised booking, retain earlier route/airport/price as history and use the current issued version for counting; do not add old and new segments or reissued fares. Keep airports distinct and do not invent transfers.
- A long reply thread can quote several stays or years. Keep each provider statement with its own date context; link an attachment amount only to the stay actually billed. A later survey may supply arrival while a folio supplies departure, but property/date coincidence alone does not prove the same room/order. Multi-room dates count once and a voucher adjustment already in the stated net total is not subtracted again.
- Match explicit cancellations across authorized accounts to their booking identity before recognizing an old confirmation. Preserve both versions while counting the current canceled order as zero stay nights. A warning that a card may be invalid or a booking may be canceled is not itself cancellation; zero cancellation fee is not zero-cost completed lodging. Reward-night points are noncash, and incidental parking prices are not the reservation charge.
- Boarding passes and insurance confirmations may accompany an itinerary and two passengers. Preserve their context without creating more flight segments or claiming actual boarding from a generated boarding pass. Insurance is separately reviewable ancillary material outside the hotel/flight/dining v1 kinds; a premium already included in the order invoice is not added again.
- A dining calendar reminder can display another timezone. Compare restaurant-local time with the labeled calendar timezone before declaring a change or another meal. Menu promotions, deposits for large groups and private-room minimum spend are not the current reservation amount; a generic review invitation does not prove the meal or a Michelin award.
- If a connector's decoded body is garbled, inspect MIME alternatives or supplied original bytes; when those cannot recover it reliably, read a PDF/image attachment. Do not guess at repairing lossy replacement characters. XML/OFD alternatives unsupported by a connector remain accounted for; use the corresponding readable PDF when available and state what was actually inspected.

These are tested layout lessons, not a claim that every order type, revision or channel has been validated. Keep discovery coverage, successfully extracted fields and unresolved materials separate for each account.

Staff-sent bills may have only the property name as the subject (for example, “JW Marriott Cusco”), with “bill” or “folio” only in the body and PDFs attached. For an identified property, search its name and observed hotel sender/domain with `has:attachment`, then read the actual bill. Keep lodging and incidentals folios associated with one stay; two attachments are not two stays. A return stay mentioned in an email can have a later, separate folio. Identify the bill currency separately from a header's quoted nightly-rate currency, and do not claim paid/settled when credit is zero and balance remains positive.

A guest-service email titled only “账单” or “帳單” can contain a signature shared by several hotels. Keep its PDF in the inventory and use the property printed on the folio, including a logo header when necessary. Record the labeled arrival and departure dates, and use the charge total rather than the payment or balance. If a reservation in another authorized mailbox names the same property and dates but has a different confirmation number, preserve it as a possible relationship until the correspondence is established.

eFolio can be an inline plain-text bill with no attachment. Include observed senders such as `efolio@fairfieldinn.com`, “billing summary”, “Accommodation invoice” and “Thanks for staying” when discovering hotel receipts. Extract “Dates of stay” separately from mail timestamps. Preserve voucher payments, card payments, rebates and balance as distinct components; a service charge followed by an equal rebate is not net consumption. In mixed HTML/plaintext bodies, ignore `<meta>` booking-date attributes when they conflict with or obstruct the visible labeled stay dates. OCR can separate entire label and value columns: verify their layout or use explicitly stated invoice dates rather than pairing adjacent text blindly.

Domestic flight orders can have the subject “出票成功确认” without “机票”, “航班”, “reservation” or “itinerary”. Include localized ticket-issuance subjects and observed airline sender domains. Air China orders can contain Shenzhen Airlines (`ZH`) or other carriers: read the flight table instead of deriving the carrier from the sender. Ignore literal `null` terminal placeholders. Capture an order's total once and associate it with its segments; do not charge each segment the whole order amount. A ticket-issuance email confirms ticketing, not actual boarding.

Read MIME alternatives when plain text is empty, damaged or boilerplate. Hilton SALT survey plaintext can contain only an “Invite” placeholder while the HTML has the property and checkout date. Strip scripts/styles without executing them. Do not follow survey, check-in, password, payment or booking-management links merely to extract records.

Evidence treatment:

- Reservation confirmation supports reserved status. Online check-in availability alone does not establish an actual stay.
- “Your Room is Ready” / “房间已为您备妥” explicitly establishes that the hotel prepared a room, and can corroborate a reservation or online check-in. Preserve that evidence. If it still asks the guest to arrive or collect keys, it does not alone establish arrival or completion. Use stated stay dates, not the notification date.
- A property-specific final receipt, folio or explicit post-stay survey can corroborate completion, subject to traveler identity. A general customer-service survey cannot.
- Keep invoice issue/payment/mail dates separate from arrival, departure, flight and dining dates. A survey may establish checkout only: keep arrival unknown.
- Reconcile confirmations, modifications, cancellations, receipts and questionnaires using explicit reservation identity when available, retaining the evidence lineage privately. Property/date coincidence alone cannot safely resolve different rooms or overlapping orders.
- Hotel-issued receipts can include dining and other incidental charges. Do not classify every line as room spending or as a separate restaurant reservation.

## Multiple mailboxes

Use only accounts the user authorizes and the connector actually exposes. Inspect account-selection capabilities before claiming multi-account access. If no selector is available, identify the visible mailbox from returned metadata and report the limitation. User authorization and OAuth connection are separate; a skill cannot bypass either.

Some Gmail integrations route accounts with a `link_id` connection argument that may not appear in the summarized tool description. When a connection identifier is available from an authorized, successful tool invocation, verify it with the profile tool and keep that same connection on searches, reads and attachments. Do not guess connection identifiers, treat a `to:` search as account selection, or ask users to reconnect merely because a call defaulted to the primary account.

Give each mailbox a stable opaque namespace. New record source identities should derive from mailbox namespace plus immutable message ID; retain an established export identity when extending earlier exports. Never reset IDs to evade duplicates. Forwarded copies and identical attachments across accounts need evidence-based deduplication; do not merge merely because the hotel/date matches. Do not include addresses or OAuth credentials in shared examples.

## Spending ledger

The current `fotonest.travel` v1 schema has no money fields. Preserve it. Return a separate `*.expenses.review.json` ledger under [the review-ledger schema](expenses-review.schema.json), plus CSV when useful; do not claim the app imports money until its consumer supports that contract. Validate with `python3 scripts/validate_expenses.py FILE` (requires jsonschema).

For a travel CSV, join `spending.recognized` in the reconciliation review to travel rows using `record_ids`. Include numeric amount, ISO currency and a readable basis such as final bill, documented payment or accepted booking quote. Leave amount and currency blank when no amount is recognized, including pending evidence. Use zero only when the recognized amount is explicitly zero.

Count each financial unit once. If one payment covers several travel rows, put it on one associated row and identify the shared scope so other rows do not repeat it. Use separate currency columns or a linked spending detail CSV when a single row has recognized amounts in multiple currencies. Check the CSV sums by currency against the recognized amounts in the exported scope.

Record decimal amounts as strings, explicit ISO currency or null, amount type, stable source/material IDs and a short supporting excerpt. Separate bill_total, invoice_total, payment_entry, balance, deposit_entry, refund_entry and credit_total. A credit total can include transfers or adjustments rather than cash payment. Balance zero is not zero cost. Preserve unknown currency for ambiguous `$`/`¥`; never infer currency solely from a hotel's country. Keep loyalty points separate from money; do not convert them to currency.

Exact repeated attachments can be deduplicated by hash. A hotel folio, tax invoice and card receipt can describe the same spend: preserve their relationship and do not add them together. Transferred folios/multiple rooms may also repeat charges. After establishing that charges/components are independent, their amounts may add within the same currency, including multiple rooms; keep the contributing document identities. Do not add payments/balances again to billed consumption, or combine currencies. Count stay nights separately by a date union, not by room/order count. Extract final totals only from clearly labeled totals; avoid nightly rates, cancellation penalties or promotional prices as actual spending. Unreadable fields remain pending, with no fabricated total.

For users who explicitly accept reservation quotes as consumption prices, apply the optional [spending recognition profile](stay-acceptance.md#optional-booking-price-spending-recognition). The quote can contribute to recognized spending while retaining its original evidence kind. This changes pricing recognition, not whether the document proves payment or physical attendance.
